## hack.lu 2026 – Join Our Thursday Evening Hackathon!

**Thursday, 22 October 2026 – 19:00**

This year, we're organising a hackathon during hack.lu!

With so many contributors, developers, and enthusiasts from different open-source and cybersecurity projects physically gathered at hack.lu, we thought it would be a great opportunity to bring everyone together for an evening of collaborative hacking.

Whether you're working on an existing open-source project, want to contribute to another one, or have a new idea you'd like to explore, **this is the perfect opportunity to meet fellow hackers, exchange ideas, write code, and collaborate in person.**

Bring your laptop, your ideas, and your enthusiasm. Everyone is welcome.

**Registration is required** to help us estimate the number of participants and organise the hackathon accordingly. Please register in advance so we can prepare the space and make the most of our evening together.
