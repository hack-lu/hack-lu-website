### Name references

- **Rick Deckard, Rachael, Roy Batty, Pris, and Voigt-Kampff** — *Blade Runner*, inspired by Philip K. Dick’s *Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?*
- **John Isidore and Penfield Mood Organ** — *Do Androids Dream of Electric Sheep?*
- **Bob Arctor** — *A Scanner Darkly*.
- **Joe Chip and Ubik** — *Ubik*.
- **Juliana Frink** — *The Man in the High Castle*.
